1. Who we are

Orbit Out: Mayday Rescue (the “game”, package name com.shreehari.orbitout) is a puzzle game for Android published on Google Play by ShreeHari (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is collected when you play the game, by whom, and what choices you have.

2. Information we collect

We do not run our own servers for the game and we do not receive personal information from it. The game keeps the following data only on your device, so that you can continue where you left off:

  • your progress (levels, stars, credits, rewards, streaks and achievements);
  • your settings (sound, music, haptics, reminders, accessibility options);
  • whether you bought Remove Ads.

This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game, and you can clear it at any time with Settings > RESET PROGRESS.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. You may see banner ads, an ad between some levels or restarts, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example for a free scan, a revive or double rewards).

We do not sell your personal information. In some places, personalized advertising through Google may count as “sharing” personal information under local law; section 4 explains how to turn it off.

4. Consent and your ad choices

  • Consent message. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the game shows Google’s consent message before any ad is requested, and ads follow your choice.
  • Change your choice. Where this applies, Settings > PRIVACY CHOICES in the game opens the message again at any time.
  • Advertising ID. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the phone). Without it, ads are no longer personalized.
  • Google ad settings. You can review and control ad personalization at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Remove Ads. The optional purchase removes banners and the ads between levels and restarts. Rewarded ads stay available, but only when you choose to watch one.

5. In-app purchase

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details; we never see or store your card or account information. Google Play tells the game which product you bought, so the game can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall. Google Play’s terms and Google’s privacy policy apply to the payment.

6. Reminders

The game can show one optional reminder a day (for example when the daily gift or daily mission is ready). Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no information is sent to us or to anyone else to create them. The game asks for Android’s notification permission only after you clear your first daily mission. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings > REMINDERS or in your phone’s notification settings.

7. Game engine and Google Play

  • The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may collect limited technical information, such as the device model, operating system and crash details, to keep the engine working. See Unity’s privacy policy for players.
  • Google Play may give us aggregated crash and performance reports (Android vitals) and install statistics. These reports do not identify you.

8. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and ads in the game are not set up for children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and that data about them was collected, contact us and we will help, including by asking Google to remove it where possible.

9. Storage, security and deletion

  • On your phone: your game data stays there until you reset progress or uninstall the game.
  • In transit: the ad and purchase services used by the game send data over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Held by Google: Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention rules. You can delete or reset your advertising ID and use Google’s tools to manage your data.
  • Where data is processed: Google may process data in countries other than your own, with the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not store personal information on our own servers, most requests are fulfilled through the controls above and Google’s tools. You are always welcome to contact us, and you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Changes to this policy

If the game or this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game’s store listing or release notes.

12. Contact

For any question or request about privacy in Orbit Out: Mayday Rescue, contact ShreeHari at shree.hari.solution.online@gmail.com.
